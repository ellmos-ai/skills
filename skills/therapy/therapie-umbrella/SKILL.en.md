# Therapy / Counseling — Umbrella

## Purpose

Entry point for the "Therapy / Counseling" family. Bundles overall routing and directs to the appropriate skill for specialized cases. Three active entry-point skills form the front; behind them lies a longer tail of deregistered specialized methods reachable via `code-skill-index` (`catalog-therapy.md` catalog).

## Members & Routing

| Skill | Purpose | When to use this instead of others |
|-------|---------|------------------------------------|
| `/stabilization-techniques` | Crisis intervention, grounding, safe place, PMR, panic, Window of Tolerance | **First** in acute stress/crisis — stabilization before methodology |
| `/guideline-therapies-overview` | Overview of guideline therapies: CBT, ACT, Schema Therapy, Exposure, Systemic, Psychodynamic | When choosing or explaining the appropriate **method** |
| `/counseling-basics` | Conversation techniques: active listening, mirroring, validation, MI/OARS, circular questions | When focusing on **HOW to converse**, not on the therapy method |
| (deregistered special skills) | Individual techniques (genogram, exposure details, positive psychology, …) | When a specific individual procedure is needed in depth → via `code-skill-index` |

> Routing rule: acute crisis → `/stabilization-techniques` · choose/explain method →
> `/guideline-therapies-overview` · conversation technique → `/counseling-basics` · deep individual procedure →
> deregistered specialized skill via `code-skill-index`.

## Well-Coupled Combinations

- `/stabilization-techniques` (first, acute) → `/guideline-therapies-overview` (subsequently, medium-term):
  first establish safety/Window of Tolerance, then choose the matching guideline therapy.
- `/counseling-basics` accompanies **both** — the counseling attitude (MI/OARS, validation) supports through
  stabilization as well as methodical work.

## Common Conventions

- Do not replace medical diagnosis; work psychoeducationally and resource-oriented.
- Window of Tolerance as guiding principle: stabilize first during hyperarousal, do not confront.
- Read live files of individual skills before application — this umbrella does not reproduce content.
