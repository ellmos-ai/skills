# Academic Study and Deadline Management (English)

This skill provides systematic support for academic study organisation, deadline tracking, and exam planning.

## 1. Overview and Objectives

- **Deadline and Exam Control:** Creation of binding study and exam schedules.
- **Compliance and Verification:** Alignment with official examination regulations and module handbooks.
- **Privacy Compliance:** Strict separation of personal data from academic tasks.

## 2. Workflow and Steps

1. **Clarify Goal:** Identify target exam dates or submission deadlines.
2. **Assess Current State:** Review examination regulations, LMS, and past progress.
3. **Live Verification:** Cross-check current dates on official institution websites.
4. **Schedule Building:** Backward planning from submission dates with buffer time.
5. **Output Generation:** Render a structured schedule summary table.

## 3. Mandatory Rules and Constraints

- **Official Sources First:** In case of conflict, official examination rules prevail.
- **No Credential Storage:** Never store portal logins or authentication codes.
- **Enforce Buffer Time:** Every plan must include contingency buffers.
