# Exam Preparation and Self-Testing (English)

This skill provides frameworks for generating mock exams, timed practice tests, and error analysis.

## 1. Overview and Objectives

- **Mock Exam Generation:** Creating realistic questions matching exam formats.
- **Error Diagnosis:** Identifying conceptual gaps versus oversight errors.
- **Time Management:** Practicing under realistic exam time constraints.

## 2. Workflow and Steps

1. **Question Design:** Formulate items based on course requirements.
2. **Simulation:** Complete the test without reference materials.
3. **Objective Grading:** Score answers strictly against evaluation rubrics.
4. **Targeted Revision:** Reforce weak topics highlighted by test results.

## 3. Mandatory Rules and Constraints

- **Strict Scoring:** Do not award credit for incomplete or vague answers.
- **Diagnostic Focus:** Use tests strictly to guide further study.
