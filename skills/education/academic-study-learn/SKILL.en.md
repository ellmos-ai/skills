# Active Learning and Knowledge Synthesis (English)

This skill provides methods for active study, conceptual synthesis, and structured knowledge consolidation.

## 1. Overview and Objectives

- **Active Recall and Retrieval:** Techniques for solidifying long-term memory.
- **Feynman Technique:** Simplified explanations to reveal understanding gaps.
- **Structured Note-Taking:** Creating high-quality summaries and concept maps.

## 2. Workflow and Steps

1. **Source Analysis:** Extract central arguments and key terms from text.
2. **Self-Explanation:** Explain the concept from memory in your own words.
3. **Gap Identification:** Check imprecise points against the original source.
4. **Synthesis:** Produce a concise summary note or study sheet.

## 3. Mandatory Rules and Constraints

- **Comprehension Over Memorisation:** Prioritise logical understanding.
- **Validated Sources:** Rely exclusively on verified academic literature.
