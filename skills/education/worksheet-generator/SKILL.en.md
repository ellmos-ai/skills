# Worksheet and Educational Material Generator (English)

This skill generates differentiated worksheets and practice exercises for educational and therapeutic support.

## 1. Overview and Objectives

- **Adaptive Task Design:** Tasks tailored to target age groups and difficulty levels.
- **Didactic Structure:** Clear instructions and teacher answer keys.
- **Flexible Output:** Structured rendering in Markdown, HTML, or DOCX.

## 2. Workflow and Steps

1. **Define Objective:** Specify educational goal without personal data.
2. **Adjust Level:** Set target age and difficulty parameters.
3. **Draft Generation:** Produce worksheet layout and exercise items.
4. **Review:** Verify pedagogical appropriateness prior to printing.

## 3. Mandatory Rules and Constraints

- **Strict Privacy:** Operate solely with abstract level parameters.
- **Teacher Review Required:** Materials are working drafts requiring instructor review.
